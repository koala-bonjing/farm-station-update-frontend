🌾 Heritage & Roots

Four Decades of Empowering Bicolano Farmers

FarmStation began its journey in 1982 under the name JL Comprada Fertilizer & Chemicals. Through steadfast dedication to fair pricing, technical expertise, and genuine relationships with local farmers, it has grown into Bicol's premier one-stop agrivet retail network.

1982

Founded as JL Comprada

Started supplying quality fertilizers and chemicals in Bicol to support local rice and crop producers.

2000s

Rebranded to FarmStation Agrivet

Expanded into a comprehensive one-stop agrivet store offering feeds, biologics, and modern veterinary medicines.

Today

52+ Branches & Expanding

Bicol's most accessible agrivet network, pioneering professional agronomic guidance and livestock care.
