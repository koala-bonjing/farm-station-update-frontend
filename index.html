Built in Partido, Serving Every Bicolano Farming Community

Farm Station Inc. traces its roots to Goa, Camarines Sur, beginning as a dedicated local agricultural and veterinary supply store before incorporating in 2012. Through dependable stock availability, technical field guidance, and fair trade pricing, we have grown into the region's most accessible agri-vet retail network.

ORIGIN

Partido Agricultural Supply Roots

Established in Goa, Camarines Sur to provide local rice, corn, and backyard livestock growers with authentic fertilizers and crop protection.

2012

Incorporated as Farm Station Inc.

Expanded into a full-line agri-vet store integrating commercial feeds, veterinary pharmaceuticals, biologics, and specialty seeds.

TODAY

52+ Branches Across Six Provinces

Serving thousands of farmers, poultry raisers, and pet owners daily across Camarines Sur, Albay, Sorsogon, Camarines Norte, Catanduanes, and Masbate.
