# Official GSAP Skills Suite

This skill bundles the official GreenSock Animation Platform (GSAP) skills:

- **gsap-core** (`../gsap-core/SKILL.md`): Core tweens (`gsap.to`, `from`, `fromTo`, `set`), easing, staggers, and `gsap.matchMedia()` for responsive & `prefers-reduced-motion` safety.
- **gsap-timeline** (`../gsap-timeline/SKILL.md`): Sequencing, position parameters, labels, nested timelines, and playback control.
- **gsap-scrolltrigger** (`../gsap-scrolltrigger/SKILL.md`): Scroll-linked animations, scrubbing, pinning, batching, and entrance reveals.
- **gsap-performance** (`../gsap-performance/SKILL.md`): 60fps GPU-accelerated transforms (`x`, `y`, `scale`, `autoAlpha`), avoiding layout thrashing, and `will-change` discipline.
- **gsap-plugins** (`../gsap-plugins/SKILL.md`): Flip, SplitText, ScrollSmoother, Observer, Draggable, and MorphSVG patterns.
- **gsap-utils** (`../gsap-utils/SKILL.md`): `gsap.utils.toArray()`, `clamp()`, `mapRange()`, `interpolate()`, `snap()`, and `context()`.
- **gsap-react** & **gsap-frameworks** (`../gsap-react/SKILL.md`, `../gsap-frameworks/SKILL.md`): Lifecycle-safe `useGSAP()` and framework cleanup.
