# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

HTML5, modern CSS3 (transforms, Grid, flexbox), vanilla JS / GSAP

## Users

Customers seeking fresh, local, organic produce, farm-to-table goods, and seasonal harvest market updates.

## Product Purpose

Modern, fast, accessible storefront and showcase website for Farm Station, communicating freshness, locality, and sustainable agriculture while making produce catalogues and visit/order information effortless to browse.

## Positioning

Farm-fresh agricultural authenticity paired with modern, buttery-smooth digital craft—moving away from generic supermarket tropes or sterile SaaS layouts.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile shoppers checking weekly harvest availability, farm store hours, CSA subscriptions, and location directions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mobile-first responsive layouts down to 390px.
- Zero layout shift (CLS < 0.1) for image/produce catalogs.
- High-contrast accessible text (WCAG 2.2 AA/AAA) on organic backgrounds.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all spatial animations.

## Product Principles

1. **Earth & Freshness First:** Ground all visuals, color tokens, and typography in real agricultural materials (deep leaves, fresh soil, golden harvest).
2. **Frictionless Utility:** Hours, location, produce availability, and order CTAs must be instantly legible without cognitive clutter.
3. **Buttery-Smooth & Lightweight:** 60fps GPU acceleration, native CSS over heavy JavaScript frameworks (Ponytail + Boost principles).
